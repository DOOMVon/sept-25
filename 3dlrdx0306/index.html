Happy first anniversary!!!

I still think about how we met at 3C. Honestly, I didn't expect anything to happen back then. I was still carrying a lot (lol), and I didn't think I was ready for anyone. Then somehow we started hanging out almost every day, and without me noticing, you became the person I wanted to see the most.

I don't know if I've ever told you this properly, but being with you feels light. Around you I don't feel like I'm carrying a burden anymore. I started actually enjoying my life again, and a big part of that is because of you.

I remember buying matching socks at McD, staying up late at BM Coffee, falling asleep in your car, going to the gym together, and making TikToks. They're all small things, but they're the ones that stuck with me. Those are the moments I think about when I think about us.

I know this year wasn't perfect. I know I'm not always the boyfriend you deserve, and there are things I need to work on, like being more present, communicating better, or even being more understanding. I'm not writing this to make excuses. I'm writing it because I want you to know I see it, and I want to do better for you.

Thank you for staying, for being patient with me, and for making this year one of the best parts of my life.

I love you. Here's to our second year, and many more after that.

yours,
